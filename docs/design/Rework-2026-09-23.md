  have difference purposes (selling items to make money/ breaking to craft)"*.

  essence**, so one T40 essence ≈ **2500** buy value, **sells at /25 ≈ 100**. A Common 20× cheaper

- **Recipes need essence as well as mats.** At **100%**: T80 weapon **2000**, T40 weapon **400**; T80

item). His note names overenchant breaking as an IG essence source.
