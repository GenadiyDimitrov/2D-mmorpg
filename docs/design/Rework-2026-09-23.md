### 2.4 Essence — the breaking economy (his rule, 2026-09-23)

- **Breaking a Common or Mythic item gives its grade's essence** (one essence per metal grade; the
  names wait on the §2.2.4 naming decision).
- **The amount is AUTHORED ONCE per item, proportional to its price at authoring time, never a live
  formula.** *"changing prices later should not change the essence amount"*.
- **A Mythic breaks for 100% of its price-worth; a Common breaks for 70%.** *"breaking and selling to
  have difference purposes (selling items to make money/ breaking to craft)"*.
- His worked example (explicitly *"dont quote me"* on the prices): T40 2H Mythic ≈ 5kk → **2000
  essence**, so one T40 essence ≈ **2500** buy value, **sells at /25 ≈ 100**. A Common 20× cheaper

- **Recipes need essence as well as mats.** At **100%**: T80 weapon **2000**, T40 weapon **400**; T80

(`GameConstants.VendorSellFraction`), so breaking a Mythic (100% in essence) and selling it (30% in
gold) really are different choices, and selling the essence itself (1/25 = 4%) is the worst of the

item). His note names overenchant breaking as an IG essence source.
