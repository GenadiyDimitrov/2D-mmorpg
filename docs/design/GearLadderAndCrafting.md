### Weapon M.Atk is now the endgame lever
Since M.Atk went weapon-based (see §3), the staff/wand M.Atk column IS the mage endgame dial. Current
staff M.Atk: t20=79 → t76=193 (only 2.4×) — too flat for a weapon-based model on its own, but it's
carried by the robe `M.Atk ×1.17` + attribute `matk max 15%`. When S-grades are added, their M.Atk sets
the real endgame ceiling. **Tune endgame magic by authoring S-grade staff M.Atk, not by touching the
stat multiplier.**

## 2. Crafting / blueprints (NEW system — design)

Endgame gear needs **mats + a BLUEPRINT (recipe) that unlocks the craft, consumed once per craft**.

- **Blueprint = a consumable item** that (a) *unlocks* the ability to craft a specific item (learned/known
  list) and (b) is **consumed each time you craft** — so a repeatable craft needs repeated blueprints
  (a gold/mat sink + a market for blueprints).
- **Split by level/grade:** low/mid gear is craftable from mats + a cheap/common blueprint; **endgame
  (S/S\*/S\*\*) requires a rare blueprint** (boss/instance/quest drop) on top of mats.
- **Data:** a `CraftRecipe(resultItemId, blueprintItemId, Mat[] (itemId, qty), gold, craftLevelReq)` catalog
  in `Game.Shared` (mirrors `MobCatalog`/`ItemCatalog` shape). Blueprints are ordinary `ItemDef`s
  (stackable, tradable) with a `Blueprint` category/flag.
- **Server flow:** a `Craft(recipeId)` command → validate known-blueprint + mats + gold + level → consume
  mats + **one blueprint** + gold → grant result (rolls attributes per the item's tier). Single-writer,
  same pattern as everything else.
- **Learning + fuel = BOTH (owner confirmed 2026-07-25).** A recipe is LOCKED until you spend **1
  blueprint to UNLOCK it** (learn → persisted known list, the existing `DropOnly` learn step). Then
  **every craft consumes 1 more blueprint** (a `RecipeInput`) + mats. So the **first craft costs 2
  blueprints** (unlock + craft), each subsequent craft costs 1. Blueprint = an ordinary stackable/tradable
  `ItemDef`. Implementation: gate the `DropOnly` learn on consuming a blueprint, and add
  `RecipeInput(blueprintId, 1)` to the recipe's inputs.
- **Ties into:** the existing gold sink / vendor economy, `docs/design/crafting-economy` (older design),
  and the deferred castle/vault work.

## 3. What IS built (the M.Atk rework this came from)

- **M.Atk is now MULTIPLICATIVE like P.Atk** (`Entity.RecomputeDerived` + `StatCalculator`): weapon M.Atk
  is the base, the ATK stat MULTIPLIES it, no additive stat floor. Fixes the low-level inflation (a lvl-1
  mage read ~40 / shown ~72; now internal **8**, L2-exact) and the low-level one-shot (lvl-8 nuke 399→154).
- **Two stat multipliers** (owner's "2 coefficients"): `PAtkStatMult` LINEAR (physical), `MAtkStatMult`
  super-linear `(atk/40)^1.75` (magic = "INT is king"). Measured: the exponent barely moves *geared*
  endgame (ATK doesn't grow much with gear) — its real effect is rewarding ATK stat investment
  (dyes/swaps). ⚠ makes +ATK swaps scale magic hard; flag if it needs a cap.
- **Endgame lands on the original anchors**: lvl-85 mage now 414 dmg vs a high-level tank (anchor
  300–400) and ~3.8 casts to kill (anchor 2–3), vs the old drifted 578 / 2.7. No "restore" needed — the
  endgame will be set by the S-grade staff M.Atk in §1.
- **M.Atk display** = `min(internal, 20·√internal)`: shows the honest small number low, shrinks only the
  cosmic high end.
- **Mob M.Def** coefficient 3.0→3.16 (L2 lvl-83 = 262). **Mob SP** = flat 1/20 of exp (was a decaying
  curve). *(these two + the display are separate small tweaks in the same batch)*

### Residual (smaller, after the ladder is set)
- lvl-8 first-nuke still one-shots a same-level mob (154 vs 91 HP) — partly mob HP being low (91 vs L2's
  125); revisit low mob HP + first-nuke power once the ladder is locked.
