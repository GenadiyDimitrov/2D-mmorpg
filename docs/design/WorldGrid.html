BL-346 · open-world grid · map editor

Vardelar World Grid

"I want the map to be in a grid order ... each cell to be its own zone, several zones can be a castle territory ... a real open world map."

Each cell on the board is a 10k square. Every 5 × 5 cells form one 50k zone; the zone is only a line on the board, not something you edit. Pick a cell to give it a biome, levels and territory, then open it large to place spawners from the mob roster, trees, rocks and scatter areas. The board starts as an import of today's real world, with all 125 camps in place.

1 · the board

Cells and borders

Click a + to add a cell at the edge, or click a cell to edit it. Borders between two cells are open (the biomes blend), a passage (a wall with a gap) or a wall. Cells with the same territory share an outline colour. The faint lines mark the 50k zones. To change several cells at once, Shift- or Ctrl-click them (or turn on Select several on a phone). A side with no cell next to it is the edge of the map: a wall.

3 · reference

Today's 48k world, under a grid

Grid
–to walk across one cell at 250 speed
–cells today's content touches
–cells the biggest city's safe zone spans

Read off the code at 0.270: 6 towns (Towns.cs), 31 generated fields (WorldPlan), 3 hand-placed areas. Field boxes are their bounds, coloured by Biomes.cs. Dungeons live in the negative quadrant and are left out.

Why 10k cells

A city's safe zone is 4,000 to 7,000 units across and a field is 3,000 to 5,000, so at 10k either one fits inside a single cell, and a 50k zone is 25 cells. At 5k a city spans four cells and a zone is 100 cells to fill in. Placing spawners doesn't depend on cell size, because the cell editor places them anywhere inside a cell.

4 · what the board becomes

From board to game

On the boardIn the game
Cell + nameA zone id over a square of the world; teleports and the map window name it.
BiomeThe ground colour and the props it scatters (PropScatter's per-biome tables). Biomes.cs moves onto the cell.
SpawnerA SpawnZone: position, radius, roster, count, level band, rank. Today these come from WorldPlan's bearing-and-distance generator.
Prop / scatter areaHand-placed props and density overrides on top of the biome's own scatter. Props are cosmetic; the server never sees them.
TerritoryThe castle that owns these cells, ready for castles and the vault.
Wall / passageGameplay, not paint: the server needs movement collision and mob pathing around them. This is the one new system.
LaterA generator in tools/ reads these rows and writes the world data, the way --gen-faces writes SkillFaces.g.cs. You edit here, or tell Claude "update cell 2,3".
