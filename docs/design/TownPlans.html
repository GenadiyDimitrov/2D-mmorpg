Two town plans: a Y town and an X city

Drawn to scale in world units, north up, the town centre at 0,0. Grey walls are the town's octagon (the one the gate guards already stand on since BL-310). Roads are 220-280 wide, buildings stand back from the road with a dashed side path to their door, and every NPC (teal dot) stands at a door, not in the road.

Grouping is yours: weapons + armour in one shop, the Apothecary with the essence seller, the Keeper in his own house, the Master Crafter with his Anvil, and a church in the big cities for the class master and the Mindwright.

Before I build · your call

Five questions

1. Are walls solid?

Visual only means footprints drawn on the ground (like the field overlay), and you can walk through them. Solid means the server needs collision and pathing around buildings, for players, mobs and auto-hunt, which is a much bigger job. My pick: visual first, solid later with the 3D models (BL-281).

2. Which towns get which shape?

My pick: the three major cities (Brackenford, Greymarsh, Frostmere) are X, and Stonewatch and Ironreach are Y. A Y town's gates should face where its fields are, so each Y is rotated to fit its own map.

3. Do the major cities grow?

Greymarsh and Frostmere are radius 2000 today. That fits a Y, but an X with four quarters is cramped at that size. My pick: 3000 for the major cities (Brackenford is already 3500). Check that the fields around them still clear the wall.

4. Door or counter?

In this sketch the NPC stands at the door, facing the side path, and you never go inside. My pick: the door, because interiors need rooms, walls and a camera that works indoors.

5. Where does the Huntmaster stand?

Here he has a lodge by one gate. Keep him beside the Gatekeeper on the plaza (as today), or give him the lodge? This also settles the open question of whether he stays in the minor towns.

What building it touches
  • A TownLayout per town in Game.Shared: road polylines, building polygons and door points. NPC positions come from the doors, so the label-spacing asserts keep working.
  • The client draws roads and footprints flat on the ground, the way it draws fields today, so it needs a new APK. The models come later with BL-281.
  • The guards move to 3 or 4 gates. Spawn points and the respawn spot move to the plaza.
