Install on Android

  1. Tap Download above. The file is called Vardelar.apk.
  2. Open it when the download finishes (from the notification, or from Files → Downloads).
  3. Android will ask about “unknown apps”. That is normal for a game that is not on Google Play: tap Settings, allow your browser, then go back and tap Install.
  4. Open Vardelar. The login screen already knows this server, so just register an account and make a character.

Play on Windows

  1. Download for Windows above and unzip it anywhere (right-click → Extract All).
  2. Run Vardelar.exe. Windows may warn that the app is unrecognised: click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. It opens in a window. Left-click moves and targets, right-drag turns the camera, the wheel zooms, and Alt+Enter switches to full screen. Same account as on your phone.

Updating

If the game says your client is out of date, or the Download button shows a date after your install, come back here, download again and install it over the old one. Your account and characters live on the server, so nothing is lost.

The game

  • Three races (Human, Elf and Demon), each with eight paths to choose from as you grow.
  • A world from level 1 to 90: towns, hunting grounds, bosses and dungeons.
  • Play together: parties, trading, quests, crafting, and PvP if you want it.
  • Online only: everything runs on the server, so the world keeps going without you.

Vardelar is in early testing: expect bugs, resets and big changes. Android, plus a Windows test build.
